Silicon wafer defect detection

Mar–Jul 2026

Research Intern · IEMN Laboratory, UPHF, Valenciennes, France

4 MHzexcitation bandwidth
3%hidden thickness error found
2 moahead of plan
  • Built an end-to-end workflow: 3D Elmer FEM models, laser Doppler vibrometry up to 4 MHz across 72 points, passive ambient-noise methods adapted from seismology, and Python pipelines for beamforming and localisation.
  • Traced a model–measurement mismatch to wafer thickness: the data alone moved the model from the 500 µm we were given to about 390 µm, later backed by a gauge reading.
  • Designed and 3D-printed the wafer fixture, and finished all experiments in four months against a six-month plan.

2021–present

PhD research · Computational Wave Mechanics Lab, IISc · Guide: Prof. S. Gopalakrishnan

50×faster
≤2%displacement error
